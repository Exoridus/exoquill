Strengths · risks · rules
STRENGTHS
Warm and trustworthy; makes a utility feel like a writing companion. Serif note body invites reading. Differentiates hard from cold AI tools.
RISKS
Serif can read “precious” if overused. Warm palette less obviously “local/technical.” Needs discipline to avoid stationery-brand cliché.
CHOOSE WHEN
The audience is writers, researchers, journalers — people who value calm and craft over raw speed.
DO
Use serif only for note body & display. One ink accent per screen. Keep margins generous.
DON'T
No feather logos, no medieval flourishes, no decorative serif UI labels, no second accent.

Strengths · risks · rules
STRENGTHS

RISKS

RECOMMENDATION
Lead with C · Exo Ecosystem as the primary identity, with A · Ink & Quill warmth folded into its writing surfaces.

C is the only direction that satisfies the explicit goal of belonging to a broader Exo family — a reusable hex mark, shared dark/light surfaces, restrained accents. Its one weakness, coldness, is exactly A’s strength: borrow A’s serif note body and generous reading margins inside C’s chrome and you get a tool that feels like family and a calm place to write.

IF NOT C
Backup → B. If the priority is shipping a lean, unmistakably privacy-first utility fast, B is the safest, most legible choice and the cheapest to build.

Don’t ship A alone. It’s the weakest at signaling “local AI,” but it’s the best ingredient — keep it as C’s soul.
